What is Pathways to Health?

Pathways to Health is a student-organized pre-health conference at Pensacola High School, inspired by the Harvard Undergraduate World Pre-Health Conference (HUWPC).* Our mission is to open doors — connecting ambitious students with the professionals, knowledge, and inspiration they need to pursue careers in medicine, nursing, public health, research, and beyond.

Whether you're already set on becoming a physician or simply curious about what a career in healthcare looks like, this conference is designed for you. Through keynote addresses, expert panels, and direct conversations with healthcare professionals, attendees will leave with a clearer, more inspired vision of their future.

* Pathways to Health is an independent, student-run event. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Harvard University or the Harvard Undergraduate World Pre-Health Conference.

Who can attend?

High school and college students who are curious about a career in healthcare.

Yes, parents and guardians are welcome.
