What we do

See what a career in healthcare really looks like, before you pick a major.

Pathways to Health is a one-day pre-health conference organized by students at Pensacola High School. It's for anyone thinking about a career in medicine, nursing, public health, research, or anywhere else in healthcare, whether you've known you wanted to be a doctor since you were six or you're just starting to wonder what's out there.

The day is built around keynotes, panels, and real conversations with doctors, nurses, researchers, and public health professionals. You'll hear them talk honestly about how they got where they are, ask the questions you can't get answered online, and meet people who can help with your next step.

Modeled after the Harvard Undergraduate World Pre-Health Conference.*
